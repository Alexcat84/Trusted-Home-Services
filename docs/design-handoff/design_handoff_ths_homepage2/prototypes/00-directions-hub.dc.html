My recommendation

Lead with The Promise — then bolt on the best of the rest.

In a low-trust category, the no-deposit guarantee is the single most disarming thing this business can say — and today it's buried in a sidebar. Leading with it is the most defensible, least template-like opening, and unlike the video-first route it doesn't depend on an asset that doesn't exist yet. It also reads as "impactful and professional" without a darkened stock hero.

What I'd borrow from the others:

  • The Transformation — drop its three-column video hero in as the section right below the fold, so the reserved before/after reel has a home the day it's shot.
  • The Concierge — use its interactive trades board as the /services index page; it scales past nine without a crowded dropdown.
  • The Sequence — make its day-1-to-sold roadmap the How We Work page; it's the clearest way to set expectations.
  • Two Doors + The Local — take Two Doors' audience toggle for the services section, and The Local's Ottawa neighbourhood proof as a trust band before the footer.

If the client's priority is instead "show the work immediately," swap the lead for The Transformation — same borrowed parts, just reordered. Tell me which way to consolidate and I'll build the single merged homepage.
