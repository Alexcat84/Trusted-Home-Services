Eight homepage directions for Trusted Home Services.

Three of them (Group A) share the hero the client locked down: contact on the left, the renovation video in the center, testimonials on the right. All eight are single self-contained files with no external resources, a neutral logo placeholder, the nine services only in the menu, the zero-deposit promise up front, EN, FR and ES, and a focus-trapped quote modal that opens from every quote button. My recommendation

Ship from Group A. Lead with A2 Spotlight if the video will be strong, A1 Blueprint if it needs to stand on its own.

The client fixed the hero, so the production homepage should come from Group A. Between the three, the choice hinges on one thing: the renovation video. If the AI video lands well, A2 Spotlight is the strongest frame for it. The center panel glows, the whole page is built to make that reel the star, and it matches the reserved-video vision exactly. If the video slips or reads as filler, A1 Blueprint is the safer pick. It carries the page on typography, hierarchy and the guarantee, so it looks finished even with a plain poster. A3 Daylight is the fallback when the priority is calm and readability over drama.

What I would borrow from the rest:

  • B1 The Guarantee. Its $0 headline treatment, folded into the promise strip so the differentiator hits harder without taking over the hero.
  • B5 The Cost of Waiting. Its list-as-is versus prepared comparison, dropped in mid-page as the persuasion block that turns interest into a quote.
  • B4 On the Map. Its Ottawa neighbourhood proof, as a trust section once the client supplies real addresses and photos.
  • B3 Agent Desk. Its turnaround board and realtor framing, moved to the For Realtors page where it belongs.
  • A3 Daylight. Its white space and type scale, to keep whichever Group A direction ships from feeling dense.

Group B stays as exploration. If the client reacts more to a thesis than to a treatment, we can promote one of them and rebuild its hero on the fixed three-column structure.
